THE IMPOSSIBLE TIMELINE

OFFICIAL RECORD

ESTABLISHED BY THE RECORDS

  • The rear entrance system clock was seven minutes fast.
  • The corrected access events are 19:45:31, 19:46:02, and 19:47:18.
  • The access log records credential use and door activity but does not identify the person using the credential.
  • The parking-camera index records a vehicle entering and leaving the frame, but its clock cannot be independently verified and its occupants cannot be identified.
  • Peter Shaw reported that Thomas left The Lantern alone at approximately 20:10.
  • The following morning's records document the discovery of a damaged boat at 07:10 and the entry of a watch and jacket into inventory at 08:05.

NOT ESTABLISHED

  • The identity of the person who used the rear entrance credential.
  • Whether the vehicle recorded by the parking camera was connected to Thomas.
  • Whether Peter's account is false.
  • The exact time Thomas disappeared.
  • Whether Thomas entered the lake or died there.
  • The precise sequence of Thomas's movements between The Lantern and Lake Ash.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE THE GAPS?

  • Independently calibrated camera timestamps.
  • Identifiable footage or reliable eyewitness accounts.
  • Independent records connecting a person or vehicle to the relevant locations.
  • Additional verified evidence establishing the timing and circumstances of Thomas's disappearance.

FINAL FINDING

The available records allow part of the timeline to be corrected, but they do not establish a complete sequence of Thomas Reed's movements.

Peter's account remains uncorroborated, but it is not conclusively contradicted by the supplied evidence.

The search records establish when the boat and belongings were recorded, not when Thomas disappeared or what ultimately happened to him.

The timeline remains incomplete.

CASE STATUS: OPEN

Some questions remain unanswered.
That is where the next investigation begins.
